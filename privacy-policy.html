Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Celebrownz is a bakery in Nandanam, Chennai. This page explains what information we receive when you use www.celebrownz.com or order from us, and how we use it.

What this website collects

This website does not have accounts, sign-ups or online payments, and it does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Nothing you choose on the menu or type into the “Plan your order” form is sent to us or stored by the website. That form only writes a message for you, which you can review and send yourself in WhatsApp.

What we receive when you order

Orders are placed on WhatsApp. When you message us, we receive what you choose to share, such as:

  • your name and WhatsApp number
  • the items, quantities and date you need them
  • a message to write on a customised slab
  • a delivery address, if you ask for delivery

We use this only to confirm, prepare and hand over your order, and to reply to your questions. We don’t sell your information or use it for advertising.

Sharing

If you choose delivery, we share your name, number and address with the delivery partner who brings your order. We don’t share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Services run by other companies

  • WhatsApp carries our messages. WhatsApp’s own privacy policy applies to how it handles your chats.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on this website. When the page loads, your browser requests the fonts from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Google Maps opens if you tap “Get directions”. Google’s privacy policy applies there.

How long we keep it

Order details stay in our WhatsApp chat with you. We keep them only as long as we need them to handle your order and any follow-up questions.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete our chat history with you. Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll take care of it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
